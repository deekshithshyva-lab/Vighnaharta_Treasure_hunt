How to Play

Ten steps to wisdom

1. Explore each ancient room and find the glowing treasure chest.

2. Open the chest and answer three ancient questions about Lord Ganesha stories, one at a time.

3. You get only one try per question. Get all three right to unlock the room's puja item — miss one and the room resets from question 1.

4. Finish all ten levels as quickly as possible, then watch the final puja cinematic.
